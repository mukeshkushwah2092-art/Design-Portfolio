About
Design thinking
meets real users

Hi, I'm Mukesh — a UI/UX designer with over 7 years of experience designing user-centered products across web and mobile platforms.

I've worked on projects ranging from e-commerce to enterprise dashboards, focusing on creating intuitive interfaces and seamless user journeys that serve real people in real contexts.

My approach is rooted in design thinking — I believe the best products come from deeply understanding the human on the other end. I turn complex requirements into simple, meaningful experiences that just work.

Process
How I solve
design problems
01
Discover

02
Define
Synthesize findings into clear problem statements, personas, and user journey maps that guide every decision forward.
03
Design
Ideate through sketches, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes — iterating fast and staying close to the user's mental model.
